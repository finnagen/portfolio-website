my journey

I started developing when I was barely old enough to know what I was doing using Roblox Studio; I learned Luau by teaching myself through tutorials and countless hours of scrolling through documentation, as well as getting help from people who were much better than me.

Throughout this experience, I picked up on multiple other languages, like Python and Typescript, and began music production as an extension of my previous musical skills with the piano and drums. I also began learning graphic design in Figma.
